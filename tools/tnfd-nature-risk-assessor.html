1. Entity & scope

TNFD requires disclosure by entity and, where material, at the value-chain level (TNFD Rec. Strategy.C). Start with the reporting boundary.

About the LEAP approach: TNFD's integrated assessment process. Locate interfaces with nature → Evaluate dependencies & impacts → Assess risks & opportunities → Prepare to respond and disclose. Every tab is one step.
Source: TNFD (2023), Guidance on the identification and assessment of nature-related issues: The LEAP approach, v1.1.

L Locate your interface with nature

Identify where direct operations and value-chain activities interact with ecosystems. The TNFD priority triggers are: (1) sensitive location overlap (Key Biodiversity Areas, protected areas, indigenous lands), (2) water-scarce regions, (3) degraded ecosystems. Source: TNFD LEAP Guidance §L1–L4.

L1 — Span of the business & value chain

L2 — Biome & ecosystem interface

Select the biomes your operations and value chain touch. Uses IUCN Global Ecosystem Typology v2.0 functional biomes.

L3 — Sensitive-location overlap (TNFD priority filter)

Why this matters: TNFD's Guidance for the Identification and Assessment of Nature-related Issues names sensitive-location overlap as the single strongest materiality trigger. A site in a KBA, protected area, or water-stressed basin almost always crosses the materiality threshold for disclosure, even at low revenue weight.

L4 — Upstream commodity sourcing

For food, ag, forestry, apparel: trace exposure to high-deforestation-risk commodities under EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR, effective 30 Dec 2025 for large operators).

E Evaluate dependencies & impacts

TNFD LEAP Guidance §E1–E4. Dependency ratings (High/Med/Low/Very Low) below are drawn from the ENCORE tool (Natural Capital Finance Alliance / UNEP-FI / Global Canopy) v0.3, which scores the materiality of 21 ecosystem services for 136 sub-industries. We prefill by sector; adjust based on your actual processes.

E1 — Dependencies on ecosystem services (pre-filled by sector)

Each ecosystem service is rated for how much your business depends on it. A disrupted service = operational or supply disruption.

P Prepare — response & target setting

TNFD LEAP Guidance §P1–P4. Aligns to SBTN's 5-step methodology (Assess → Interpret & Prioritise → Measure, Set, Disclose → Act → Track) and the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework Target 15.

P1 — Mitigation hierarchy

All nature responses must apply the mitigation hierarchy: Avoid → Reduce → Restore & Regenerate → Transform (last: offset). Set a position per step.
