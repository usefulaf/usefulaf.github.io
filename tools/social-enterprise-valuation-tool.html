Method 1 — Revenue Multiple (Comparable Companies)

Applies sector-specific EV/Revenue multiples to your LTM and forward revenue. Multiples below are private-market ranges compiled from Damodaran Jan 2024 data, PitchBook VC-Backed Company valuations (Q4 2024), and CapIQ impact-sector median / IQR where available. Early-stage multiples are discounted for size and risk.

Method 2 — Discounted Cash Flow (5-year + terminal)

5-year explicit forecast + Gordon Growth terminal value, discounted at stage- and geography-adjusted WACC. Growth decays linearly from your inputted Y1 rate toward a sector steady state. Margins converge linearly to the sector-typical mature EBITDA margin.

Method 3 — VC Method (Berkus / Exit-Back)

Standard VC method: project Y5 revenue → apply exit EV/Revenue multiple → discount at investor target IRR → subtract expected dilution. Used for SAFE / priced rounds to back into a defensible pre-money.

Method 4 — First Chicago (probability-weighted scenarios)

Three-scenario weighted valuation used by early-stage impact investors when exits are uncertain. Each scenario gets its own revenue trajectory, exit multiple, and discount rate. Probabilities must sum to 100%.

Impact Premium / Discount

Adjusts your weighted valuation based on (a) the depth and rigour of your impact profile, and (b) your lead investor's return expectations. Built from GIIN 2023 Annual Impact Investor Survey (N=308, $213B AUM), Acumen Lean Data research, and Bridges Ventures "Spectrum of Capital" calibration.

How to read this

An impact premium (+% on valuation) reflects that mission-aligned investors will accept lower financial return in exchange for depth of impact — but only when the impact is rigorously evidenced and intentional. A thin impact story paired with a below-market investor produces a discount: you will be priced as a worse deal than a pure-commercial comp, not a better one.
