Entity Profile & Reporting Scope

Establishes which CSRD wave applies, the value-chain scope of your assessment, and the sector context that shapes IRO identification. Required for ESRS 1 §62-63 (basis of preparation) and BP-1 (general basis).

Reference: ESRS 1 §3 Materiality Assessment; ESRS 1 §62-67 Reporting boundary; EFRAG IG 1 §3.1 Step 1 Understanding the context.

Value chain scope

ESRS 1 §63 requires the materiality assessment to cover own operations and the upstream/downstream value chain.

Why this matters for DMA: The value-chain scope decision drives where you look for IROs. EFRAG IG 1 §3.4 confirms a "tiered approach" based on relative likelihood of severe negative impacts is acceptable, but it must be justified in the BP-2 disclosure. Under-scoping the value chain is the most common audit finding in Wave 1 CSRD reports (per EFRAG Q&A March 2026 Webinar Series).

Stakeholder Engagement Map

ESRS 1 §22 distinguishes affected stakeholders (whose interests are or could be affected by your activities or value chain) from users of sustainability statements (investors, lenders, business partners). Both must be considered. Engagement evidence directly supports IRO-1 §53(a) and SBM-2 disclosures.

Reference: ESRS 1 §22-23 Stakeholders; §53 IRO-1; ESRS 2 SBM-2 §43-45 Interests and views of stakeholders. EFRAG IG 1 §3.1.2 Stakeholder mapping.

IRO Long List by ESRS Topic

Identify Impacts (actual/potential, positive/negative), Risks, and Opportunities across all 10 ESRS topical standards. EFRAG IG 1 §3.2 recommends starting from the ESRS sustainability matters list (AR 16) and pre-screening sector-specific issues using ESRS sector standards (where issued) plus SASB Standards as proxy.

Reference: ESRS 1 AR 16 Sustainability matters; EFRAG IG 1 §3.2 Identifying IROs; ESRS topical standards E1-E5, S1-S4, G1.

Entity-specific IROs are sustainability matters not covered by ESRS topical standards. ESRS 1 §11 requires their identification when material.

Impact Materiality Scoring

For each IRO, assess the severity of the impact (combination of scale × scope × irremediability per ESRS 1 §43-44) and likelihood. Negative impacts are scored regardless of probability if they occur; positive impacts use likelihood-weighted scoring. Time horizon: short ≤1y, medium 1-5y, long >5y.

Reference: ESRS 1 §43-44 Impact materiality assessment (severity = scale × scope × irremediability for negative impacts); EFRAG IG 1 §3.3.1.

Financial Materiality Scoring

For each Risk and Opportunity, assess magnitude of financial effect (5-pt scale: ≤€0.1m / 0.1-1m / 1-10m / 10-100m / >€100m), likelihood, and time horizon. ESRS 1 §50 requires financial effects to cover financial position, financial performance, cash flows, access to finance, or cost of capital.

Reference: ESRS 1 §49-51 Financial materiality assessment; EFRAG IG 1 §3.3.2.

Risks and Opportunities flagged in tab 3 appear here for scoring.

Materiality Threshold

ESRS 1 §AR 14 requires entities to define and disclose the materiality threshold used. EFRAG IG 1 §3.4 confirms thresholds are set per assessment dimension (impact and financial) and must be justified.

Reference: ESRS 1 §AR 14; EFRAG IG 1 §3.4 Setting and applying thresholds.
Default: 9 (e.g., severity 3 × likelihood 3, equivalent to "Medium-High" rating). Lower = more inclusive.
Default: 9. Some Wave 1 reporters used 12-15; some used 6 to be more inclusive.
