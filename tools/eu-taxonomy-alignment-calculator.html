EU Taxonomy Alignment Calculator Regulation 2020/852

Screen economic activities against the EU Taxonomy — Substantial Contribution (TSC), Do No Significant Harm (DNSH) and Minimum Safeguards — and compute Turnover, CapEx and OpEx alignment ratios for Article 8 Delegated Act reporting. Embeds the Climate Delegated Act (2021/2139) as amended, the Environmental Delegated Act (2023/2486), the Article 8 Disclosures Delegated Act (2021/2178), the Complementary Climate Delegated Act (2022/1214), and the Platform on Sustainable Finance’s Minimum Safeguards recommendations (Oct 2022).

Entity & reporting profile

The Article 8 Disclosures Delegated Act (2021/2178, as amended by 2022/1214 and 2023/2486) requires in-scope NFRD/CSRD undertakings and financial-market participants subject to Article 8 of Regulation 2020/852 to disclose taxonomy-aligned KPIs. Scope expands phased through 2028 under CSRD.

IFRS 15 / Directive 2013/34/EU Art. 2(5) net turnover for the year.
Additions to tangible & intangible fixed assets per IAS 16, IAS 38, IAS 40, IFRS 16, IFRS 3 — excluding fair-value changes & goodwill (2021/2178 Annex I §1.1.2.2).
Direct non-capitalised R&D, building renovation, short-term lease, maintenance & repair (2021/2178 Annex I §1.1.3.2).

Reporting scope flags

The calculator stress-tests each activity against six environmental objectives (Articles 9 & 10 of Regulation 2020/852): Climate change mitigation (CCM), adaptation (CCA), sustainable use of water (WTR), transition to a circular economy (CE), pollution prevention (PPC), and biodiversity & ecosystems (BIO). An activity must substantially contribute to at least one objective, do no significant harm to the others, and comply with minimum safeguards to be aligned.

Eligible economic activities

Select activities from the delegated acts catalogue and enter turnover, CapEx and OpEx associated with each. Activities flagged Enabling enable GHG reductions elsewhere; Transitional activities (Art. 10(2)) have time-limited thresholds. An activity is eligible once selected; aligned only if it passes Substantial Contribution + DNSH + Safeguards (see subsequent tabs).

Primary source texts: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2139 Annex I (CCM) & Annex II (CCA); 2022/1214 adds nuclear (4.26–4.28) & fossil gas (4.29–4.31); 2023/2486 adds Annexes for WTR, CE, PPC & BIO.

Substantial contribution — technical screening criteria

Each added activity is screened against the primary-objective TSC quantitative threshold or qualitative criteria. The tool is pre-loaded with the Climate Delegated Act and Environmental Delegated Act thresholds in force for reporting years 2024–2027 (as amended). Answers set the Substantial Contribution flag used in the Results tab.

Methodology note. Where an activity invokes a Life-Cycle GHG threshold (e.g. electricity generation <100 gCO2e/kWh), the tool expects a life-cycle assessment per ISO 14067 / PEF or the JRC Life Cycle Indicators Framework. Where an activity relies on a climate risk & vulnerability assessment (CR&VA) for CCA, a 30-year horizon under at least two IPCC AR6 SSPs (high and low emission) is required.

Do No Significant Harm — six-objective screen

For each activity, confirm that the DNSH criteria for the other five environmental objectives are met. Any single “No” answer disqualifies the activity from alignment even if the Substantial Contribution test is passed.

Generic DNSH Appendix references. CCA DNSH follows Annex A of Delegated Regulation 2021/2139 (climate risk screening). WTR DNSH follows Annex B (water use & marine resource impact). PPC DNSH follows Annex C (substance hazard classifications, incl. REACH Authorisation List, POPs Regulation (EU) 2019/1021, CLP Annex VI categories 1A/1B). BIO DNSH follows Annex D (impact assessment for Natura 2000, IUCN I–VI & KBAs).

Minimum safeguards — Article 18

Article 18 of Regulation 2020/852 requires alignment with: OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises, UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, ILO Core Conventions, and the International Bill of Human Rights. The Platform on Sustainable Finance’s Final Report on Minimum Safeguards (October 2022) structures compliance in four sub-areas.
