About SROI: Social Return on Investment measures the social, environmental and economic value a programme creates per £/$/€ invested. It follows the 7 principles of Social Value UK and is grounded in the Green Book, NICE, DWP and World Bank proxy databases. Both modes produce valid analyses — Quick is a strong starting point, Pro lets you customise every assumption.
