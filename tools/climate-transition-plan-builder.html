2 · GHG Inventory (Scope 1 · 2 · 3) — GHG Protocol

Boundary & method. Inventory must follow the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard (rev. 2004) and Scope 3 Standard (2011). Use the operational control approach by default unless your investor relations team has elected financial control or equity share — state which, as TPT Implementation Sub-element 1 asks for it explicitly. Scope 2 must be disclosed under both location-based (grid-average) and market-based (contractual) methods per the GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance (2015).

3 · Ambition & Targets — SBTi pathway test

The ambition test. To be "aligned" in the Climate Action 100+ Net Zero Benchmark, your near-term target must cover ≥95% of Scope 1+2, and ≥67% of Scope 3 (where Scope 3 is ≥40% of your total), meet the SBTi sectoral pathway or cross-sector 1.5°C (42% absolute by 2030 from 2020, or 4.2% p.a.), and have a net-zero year ≤2050. Long-term = ≥90% absolute reduction of all material scopes by net-zero year.
