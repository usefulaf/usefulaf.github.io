Carbon Credit Integrity Assessor

A structured, evidence-based quality assessment for voluntary carbon market (VCM) credits. Embeds the ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (10 CCPs, 2023), the VCMI Claims Code of Practice v2 (Nov 2023), and published standard/methodology-level rulings from the ICVCM CCP assessments (2024–2025). Built for buyers, issuers, auditors, intermediaries, and funders who need defensible reasoning — not marketing.

Project Setup

Enter the credit lot you are assessing. All subsequent tabs react to these inputs.

Why this matters. Project type is the single biggest predictor of integrity risk. ICVCM's 2024–2025 assessments have so far given CCP-eligible labels to ART TREES v2.0 (jurisdictional REDD+ removals), certain landfill methane methodologies, and Gold Standard Metered & Measured cookstoves — and have rejected all project-scale avoided-deforestation methodologies (VM0009, VM0015, VM0037) and most grid-connected renewable methodologies. Entering accurate setup data lets the tool apply these rulings automatically downstream.

ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (CCPs) Scoring

Score each of the 10 Core Carbon Principles on a 0–5 scale based on documentary evidence you have reviewed. Published in the ICVCM Assessment Framework (March 2024). CCPs are grouped in three categories: Governance (1–4), Emissions Impact (5–8), Sustainable Development (9–10).

Scoring convention. 0 = Not met / not evidenced · 1 = Poor · 2 = Partial · 3 = Adequate (meets ICVCM threshold) · 4 = Good · 5 = Excellent / gold-standard evidence. A CCP-eligible label from ICVCM does not automatically mean a 5 — assess the specific project's implementation.

VCMI Claims Code of Practice — Buyer Eligibility

The VCMI Claims Code of Practice (v2, Nov 2023) is the buyer-side counterpart to ICVCM. Use this tab if you (or your client) intend to make a claim using these credits. Founded by the Voluntary Carbon Markets Integrity Initiative.

Scope 3 Flexibility Claim

VCMI launched a Scope 3 Flexibility Claim pathway (Nov 2024) for companies that have SBTi-aligned targets but are struggling with Scope 3 reduction. Requires demonstrating action + disclosure, and using high-integrity (CCP) credits to close up to 50% of the near-term Scope 3 gap for a maximum of three reporting periods.
