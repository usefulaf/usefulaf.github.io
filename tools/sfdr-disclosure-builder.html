Financial Market Participant & Product Setup

SFDR applies to Financial Market Participants (FMPs) per Art 2(1) — AIFMs, UCITS management companies, MiFID portfolio managers, IORPs, insurance undertakings offering IBIPs, EuVECA/EuSEF managers — and financial advisers per Art 2(11). Each FMP discloses at entity level (Art 3, 4, 5) and at product level (Art 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11).

Reference: Reg (EU) 2019/2088 Art 2(1) FMPs; Art 2(11) Financial Advisers; Art 4 entity-level adverse impacts; Art 8 promotion of E/S characteristics; Art 9 sustainable investment objective.

Article 6 / 8 / 9 Classification

SFDR creates three product-disclosure regimes. The choice is operational (driven by what the product actually does) not a marketing label — the ESMA Supervisory Briefing on Sustainability Risks (May 2024) confirms competent authorities will challenge inconsistent classification. The Commission Q&A (May 2022 §1) is explicit: there is no "Art 8+" or "dark green Art 8" sub-category in the regulation.

Reference: Reg (EU) 2019/2088 Art 6 (sustainability risk integration baseline); Art 8 (E/S characteristic promotion); Art 9 (sustainable investment objective); Commission Q&A C(2022) 3051 final May 2022 §1; ESMA Public Statement ESMA34-1244747569-1864 (Aug 2024) on fund names with ESG terms.

Classification decision

Walk through the four gating questions below to confirm classification. Each gate must be passed (in order) for the higher classification.

Gate 1 · Sustainability risk integration in investment decisions Required for all

Art 6 baseline: every product must integrate "sustainability risk" — defined in Art 2(22) as an ESG event/condition that could cause a material negative impact on investment value.

Gate 2 · Promotion of environmental and/or social characteristics (Art 8)

Art 8 attaches when the product "promotes" E or S characteristics in pre-contractual material — but does not have a binding sustainable investment objective. Investee companies must follow good governance practices.

Gate 3 · Sustainable investment objective (Art 9)

Art 9 attaches only when the product has a binding "sustainable investment" objective per Art 2(17). Sustainable investment = (a) contributes to E or S objective + (b) does no significant harm + (c) good governance. Commission Q&A (Apr 2023 §III.1) clarifies Art 9 funds must invest 100% in sustainable investments (subject to liquidity/hedging exclusions per the Q&A).

Gate 4 · PAB / CTB or Reduction of carbon emissions (Art 9(3))

Art 9(3) applies where the product has a reduction of carbon emissions objective. Such products must have an EU Climate Transition Benchmark (CTB) or EU Paris-Aligned Benchmark (PAB) as a reference per Reg 2016/1011 amendments — or, in their absence, document a 1.5°C-aligned glide path.

Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) Indicators

Article 4 of SFDR requires entity-level disclosure of how the FMP considers principal adverse impacts (PAIs) of investment decisions on sustainability factors. RTS Annex I Table 1 enumerates 14 mandatory indicators (9 Environmental, 5 Social) that must be reported by any FMP that considers PAIs (mandatory if ≥500 employees per Art 4(3)-(4); comply-or-explain otherwise). Tables 2 and 3 list opt-in indicators — at least one additional environmental and one additional social must be reported.

Reference: Reg (EU) 2019/2088 Art 4; Reg (EU) 2022/1288 Art 6 + Annex I Tables 1, 2, 3; ESAs JC 2024 27 (Dec 2024) §3 amendments to mandatory PAIs and product-level Art 7 disclosure templates.

Score each indicator: data availability and disclosure status. Per RTS Annex I Tab 1, all 14 are mandatory if you "consider" PAIs.

At least one additional environmental indicator must be selected and disclosed (RTS Art 6(1)(b)).

At least one additional social indicator must be selected and disclosed.

Article 7 product-level PAI consideration. Each product separately discloses whether it considers PAIs. Art 8/9 products that consider PAIs at product level must disclose the indicators in Annex II/III pre-contractual templates. Products that do not consider PAIs at product level must explain why prominently — and Wave 1 ESMA reviews flagged inconsistent FMP/product PAI scoping as a top finding.

Sustainable Investment 3-Prong Test (Art 2(17))

Every investment counted toward "% sustainable investment" disclosed in Art 8/9 templates must pass three cumulative tests per Art 2(17): (a) contribution to an environmental or social objective, (b) do no significant harm (DNSH), and (c) good governance. Commission Q&A (Apr 2023 §III.1) confirms the FMP must define and disclose its methodology — there is no prescribed method, but the methodology must be applied consistently and documented.

Reference: Reg (EU) 2019/2088 Art 2(17); Reg (EU) 2022/1288 Art 16-18; Commission Q&A C(2023) 2849 final (April 2023) §III.1; ESAs Joint Q&A JC 2022 47 (Nov 2022) §IV; ESMA Supervisory Briefing on Sustainability Risks (May 2024).

Prong 1 — Contribution methodology

A "de minimis" or activity-based approach with no revenue threshold is the highest greenwashing risk per ESMA briefing; ESAs JC 2024 27 §4.2 reinforces the need for a quantitative threshold.

Prong 2 — DNSH methodology

DNSH must consider at minimum the indicators in Annex I that are relevant — RTS Art 17(1)(b) defines this as the PAI indicators in Tables 1, 2 and 3 — and must be applied with quantitative or activity thresholds.

Prong 3 — Good governance

Art 2(17) requires sound management structures, employee relations, remuneration, and tax compliance to be considered for every investee.

Pre-contractual Annex II / III Template Builder

Every Art 8 product attaches Annex II to its prospectus / pre-contractual disclosure; every Art 9 attaches Annex III. The RTS standardises the layout — 9 mandatory sections covering objectives, investment strategy, sustainable investment proportion, monitoring, methodologies, data, due diligence, engagement, and (where applicable) reference benchmark.

Reference: Reg (EU) 2022/1288 Annex II (Art 8) and Annex III (Art 9); ESAs JC 2024 27 §6.1 amendments; ESMA Q&A on UCITS prospectus and KIID Section IX.

Periodic Disclosure Annex IV / V Template Builder

Annex IV (Art 8) and Annex V (Art 9) are appended to the annual report (UCITS) or annual financial report (AIF) and disclose actual attainment over the reporting period — not commitments. ESAs JC 2024 27 §7 sharpens the requirement for actual vs. minimum/target reporting and adds GHG emissions reduction performance metrics.

Reference: Reg (EU) 2022/1288 Annex IV (Art 8) and Annex V (Art 9); ESAs JC 2024 27 §7 amendments on actual attainment + GHG performance.

EU Taxonomy Cross-walk (SFDR Art 5/6 + Reg 2020/852)

SFDR Art 5 and Art 6 require disclosure, for products that promote E characteristics (Art 8) or have E objectives (Art 9), of how and to what extent the underlying investments are in environmentally-sustainable economic activities under the EU Taxonomy Regulation. Annex II/III/IV/V templates require both turnover-based and capex-based ratios, computed at weighted-portfolio level.

Reference: Reg (EU) 2019/2088 Art 5, 6; Reg (EU) 2020/852 Art 9 (six environmental objectives); Reg (EU) 2021/2178 Art 8 Disclosures Delegated Act; Reg (EU) 2023/2486 Annexes I-IV (objectives 3-6 technical screening criteria).
